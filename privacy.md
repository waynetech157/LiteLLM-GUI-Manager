# Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled in connection with LiteLLM GUI Manager, its product website, software licensing, purchases, and support.

## 1. About LiteLLM GUI Manager

LiteLLM GUI Manager is an independently developed third-party Windows desktop application for configuring and managing LiteLLM.

LiteLLM GUI Manager is not an official LiteLLM product.

## 2. Information You May Provide to Us

Depending on how you interact with us, we may receive information such as:

- your email address;
- purchase or order information;
- license-related information;
- your Device ID when you provide it for machine-bound license activation, re-issuance, or support;
- information you voluntarily provide when contacting support.

Please do not send passwords, API keys, payment card numbers, or other unnecessary sensitive credentials to support.

## 3. Purchases and Payments

Purchases are processed by Paddle, which acts as the Merchant of Record for eligible transactions.

Paddle may collect and process information required for checkout, payment processing, taxation, fraud prevention, order fulfillment, and legal compliance.

We may receive limited order and customer information from Paddle where reasonably necessary to:

- confirm a purchase;
- provide the purchased license;
- provide customer support;
- investigate payment or licensing issues;
- comply with legal obligations.

We do not ask customers to send payment card details directly to us.

Paddle handles personal data according to its own privacy notice:

https://www.paddle.com/legal/privacy

## 4. License Activation

LiteLLM GUI Manager currently uses machine-bound license activation.

To issue or re-issue a license, we may process information such as:

- Device ID;
- license identifier;
- license type;
- purchase or order reference;
- customer email address where necessary.

Device IDs are used for license administration and are not intended to identify unrelated personal activities.

## 5. Data Stored by the Desktop Application

LiteLLM GUI Manager stores certain application data locally on your Windows computer.

Depending on the features you use, this may include:

- application settings;
- LiteLLM configuration data;
- provider and BASE URL configuration;
- `.env` configuration;
- the machine-bound license file;
- local usage statistics;
- crash logs, if generated.

Typical application data is stored under:

`%APPDATA%\LiteLLM_GUI_Manager\`

Some YAML configuration files may also be stored in the application's configuration directory.

## 6. Usage Statistics

If you enable the usage statistics feature, the current design stores statistics locally in a SQLite database.

Stored statistics may include:

- provider;
- model;
- account tag;
- token usage;
- cost;
- latency;
- request result or success state.

The current usage-statistics feature does not store:

- prompts;
- model responses;
- API keys;
- request headers.

These statistics remain local unless you choose to export, copy, back up, or otherwise transmit them.

## 7. API Keys, Environment Variables, and Configuration

LiteLLM GUI Manager may help you manage API keys, environment variables, YAML configuration, BASE URLs, and other settings used by LiteLLM or third-party providers.

You are responsible for protecting these credentials.

If you explicitly include `.env` files when creating a settings backup, the resulting backup may contain API keys or other secrets.

Such backup archives may not be encrypted. You are responsible for storing and transmitting them securely.

## 8. LiteLLM and Third-Party AI Providers

LiteLLM GUI Manager helps configure and launch LiteLLM and may facilitate connections to third-party model or API providers.

Requests sent through LiteLLM or to services such as model providers are subject to the privacy policies, terms, and data-handling practices of those third parties.

We do not control how LiteLLM, AI providers, API services, or other external services process information sent to them.

You should review the privacy policy of every third-party service you choose to use.

## 9. Product Website and GitHub Pages

At the time of this Policy, we do not intentionally add our own advertising or behavioral advertising trackers to the LiteLLM GUI Manager product website.
