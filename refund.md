# Refund Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

This Refund Policy applies to purchases of LiteLLM GUI Manager.

LiteLLM GUI Manager is currently offered as a one-time purchase for a perpetual software license.

## 1. Payment Processor and Merchant of Record

Eligible purchases of LiteLLM GUI Manager are processed by Paddle.

Paddle acts as the Merchant of Record for transactions processed through its checkout.

Refunds for Paddle-processed purchases are therefore processed through Paddle and are also subject to Paddle's applicable Buyer Terms and Refund Policy.

Paddle's Refund Policy is available at:

https://www.paddle.com/legal/refund-policy

## 2. General Refund Policy

Except where required by applicable law, purchases of LiteLLM GUI Manager are generally non-refundable once the digital product or license has been delivered.

However, this policy does not limit:

- mandatory consumer rights;
- statutory cancellation or withdrawal rights;
- rights relating to software that is materially defective, not as described, or not fit for its stated purpose;
- any refund that Paddle determines should be granted under its applicable policies.

Where mandatory local law provides greater rights, those rights will apply.

## 3. Technical Problems

If LiteLLM GUI Manager has a persistent technical problem that materially prevents you from using the features described on the product page, please contact us first so we can attempt to resolve the problem.

Support contact:

**waynetech157@outlook.com**

When contacting support, please provide information reasonably necessary to investigate the problem, such as:

- software version;
- Windows version;
- description of the problem;
- relevant error messages;
- screenshots where appropriate.

Please remove API keys, passwords, tokens, `.env` contents, and other unnecessary sensitive credentials before sending diagnostic information.

If a material defect cannot reasonably be resolved, a refund may be provided where required by applicable law or approved through Paddle's refund process.

## 4. License Activation Issues

If you are unable to activate a valid purchased license because of a licensing or Device ID issue, please contact support before requesting a refund.

We may attempt reasonable troubleshooting, license re-issuance, or device-association correction where appropriate.

A temporary activation or configuration problem does not automatically mean that the software is defective.

## 5. Change of Mind

Because LiteLLM GUI Manager is a digital software product that may be delivered and activated immediately, change-of-mind refunds are not guaranteed.

However, statutory cancellation or withdrawal rights available in your jurisdiction remain unaffected.

Paddle may also review refund requests on a case-by-case basis under its own Refund Policy.

- invalidated;
- made ineligible for future activation or re-issuance.

After a full refund, you should stop using the refunded license and associated paid software rights.

This does not affect rights that cannot legally be excluded.

Nothing in this section limits lawful chargeback or consumer-protection rights.
